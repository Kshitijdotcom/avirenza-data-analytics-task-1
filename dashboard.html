INSIGHTS & RECOMMENDATIONS
Insight 1
January was the peak revenue month at ₹1,55,536, followed by February (₹1,54,376). March dipped to ₹1,27,776 — warranting investigation.
Insight 2
Premium Tea Gift Hamper is the #1 SKU at ₹69,180 net revenue — a high-ticket item driving disproportionate value.
Insight 3
Website drives 51.2% of revenue. Instagram contributes 34.8% — a strong secondary channel worth investing in.
Insight 4
Return/Cancellation rate is 23.1% — high for D2C. Returned + Cancelled orders reduce net revenue significantly.
Insight 5
North-East region (brand's home) underperforms vs South & North — significant untapped potential in the home market.
Recommendation 1: Allocate 60% of Q2 marketing to Website SEO + Instagram reels targeting chai/wellness audiences to sustain and grow the Jan–Feb revenue levels.
Recommendation 2: Launch a North-East awareness campaign with local Guwahati/Shillong influencer partnerships and a 'hometown pride' discount to unlock the brand's home market.
